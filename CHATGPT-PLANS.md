# ChatGPT Plans and Usage

The Church Notes Extractor can be used with ChatGPT, but your experience may depend on your ChatGPT plan and current usage limits.

## Free accounts

A free ChatGPT account can be enough to try the system, but Free users have lower usage limits. File and image uploads, model usage, and other tools can have separate limits. If you reach a limit, ChatGPT may ask you to wait until usage resets or offer an upgrade.

Because this system commonly uses photo uploads, longer conversations, and GitHub-connected workflows, frequent users may find a paid ChatGPT plan more practical.

## Paid plans

OpenAI changes plans, features, and limits over time, so always check the official ChatGPT pricing page before subscribing.

As of August 2026:

- **ChatGPT Plus:** $20/month and includes higher usage limits than Free.
- **ChatGPT Pro:** intended for heavier AI use and offers substantially higher usage allowances. Current Pro options may vary by account and availability; OpenAI currently documents $100/month and $200/month Pro tiers.

You do **not** have to purchase Pro simply to use this repository. Start with the plan that fits your needs. If Free limits interrupt normal use, Plus may be enough. Pro is most appropriate for people who use ChatGPT heavily and want substantially higher allowances.

Official pricing and plan details:
